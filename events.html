UniAgent at CIKM

The ACM International Conference on Information and Knowledge Management (CIKM) hosts the AnalytiCup, its data challenge track. The first UniAgent shared tasks run as a CIKM AnalytiCup challenge, with results and findings presented at the conference.

UniAgent at INLG

The International Conference on Natural Language Generation (INLG) is the conference of the ACL Special Interest Group on Natural Language Generation. Our group has organized shared tasks at INLG before, and the second UniAgent shared task is proposed for INLG 2027.
