Agentic AI in University Administration

A shared task at the CIKM 2026 AnalytiCup, Rome, 8 to 11 November 2026.

  • Synopsis / Call
  • Shared Task
  • Important Dates
  • Sponsors

Synopsis / Call

Agentic AI systems combine large language models, information retrieval, planning, and tool use to act on behalf of users in complex digital environments. Their evaluation, however, remains difficult for lack of reproducible, bounded laboratories. A university administration is a closed-world, socio-technical environment whose tasks follow standardized, well-documented processes. Such an environment is therefore particularly suited to a Cranfield-style evaluation of AI agents. The controlled, reproducible evaluation of agentic AI in such enterprise-like settings is an underexplored yet highly relevant problem.

This shared task offers the first reusable laboratory for the controlled evaluation of agentic AI in a university environment, namely the University of Kassel. Participants build agents that solve standardized administrative tasks (e.g., travel permits and reimbursements) by retrieving the relevant regulations and planning the necessary steps, while complying with the institution's codes of conduct. The task runs as part of the CIKM 2026 AnalytiCup and is organized together with the OpenWebSearch.eu project.

For the full task definition, data, evaluation, and participation process, see the Task page. To take part, register your team on TIRA: sign up and then register to UniAgent 2026.

Shared Task

  • Agentic AI in University Administration: four task areas (business travel, privately advanced costs, internal procurement, administration of student employees) and two sub-tasks (retrieval, solving).
  • Agent in the Loop: A Shared Task on Agentic Support of Shared Tasks.
