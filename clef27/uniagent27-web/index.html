UniAgent at CLEF 2027

Synopsis/Call

Agentic AI systems combine large language models, information retrieval, planning, and tool use to act on behalf of users in complex digital environments. A university administration is a closed-world, socio-technical environment whose tasks follow standardized, well-documented processes, which makes it particularly suited to a Cranfield-style evaluation of AI agents.

UniAgent: Shared Tasks on Agentic AI in University Administration continues this line of work as an evaluation lab at CLEF 2027, building on the first rendition at the CIKM 2026 AnalytiCup. The tasks of the lab are to be announced.

We will present the lab at CLEF 2026 in Jena, 21 to 24 September 2026, and at ECIR 2027 in Southampton, 21 to 25 March 2027. Come talk to us there if you are considering taking part.
